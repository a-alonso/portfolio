Overview

Risk analysis platform that combines specialised AI with expert insurance knowledge

The opportunity — Insurers indemnity spend skyrockets when there are gaps in policies. 80% of them are repetitive ignored clauses like jurisdiction coverage. Common errors could be handled using AI that is configured on specific scenarios.

The solution — A configurable RAG AI engine that analyses the data, identifies key risk factors, and provides structured, auditable recommendations within seconds.

The approach — Developed proof of concepts with different sub-industries and experts that contrast the AI output to refine the 'robot' judgement. Accounting data security and full compliance.
