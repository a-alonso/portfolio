trii Privacy Policy

trii for iPhone · Last updated 7 October 2026

Overview

trii has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by the developer. What you keep in trii stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and is shared only with the friends you choose. The developer cannot see any of it.

No accounts and no server

trii needs no sign-up or login. The developer runs no server, and nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

No analytics, tracking or advertising

The app contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking domains and no collected data types.

Your trips

The trips you save, and your past trips in History, are stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit, in your private database), so they appear on your other devices. A trip holds its two stations, their times and time zones, the train service and its operator, and the latest live status your device received. Only you can read them there: the developer cannot. If you are not signed in to iCloud, they stay on your device only.

Friends

You can share your upcoming trips with friends who also use trii.

  • Inviting a friend: when you choose a contact, or type an email address or phone number, trii sends that address to Apple to find out whether it belongs to an Apple Account. The invitation is a link that you send yourself, by Messages, Mail or another app you choose. The addresses of the people you invite are kept with your share in your iCloud.
  • What your friends see: your upcoming trips — stations, dates and times, the train service, its operator and the live status your device received — and your trii name and picture, if you set them. Past trips are never shared.
  • What you see: your friends' upcoming trips, names and pictures, which are kept on your device. Your device does not ask any timetable service about a friend's trip: their live status comes from their device, through iCloud.
  • Stopping: you can stop sharing with a friend, or remove a friend, at any time. They are not told.

Your profile

You can give trii a name and a picture. Both are optional. They are kept on your device and in your iCloud, and your friends see them. If you set no name, your friends see the name of your Apple Account.

Contacts

If you allow access, trii reads your contacts on your device: to let you choose a friend, and to find your own contact card when you choose Fill From Contacts, by matching the name or email address of your Apple Account. Your contacts are not uploaded. Only the address you choose for an invitation leaves your device, as described under Friends.

Camera and photos

trii uses the camera only when you choose Take Photo, and your photo library only through Apple's photo picker, which gives trii just the photo you pick. The picture is used as your profile picture.

Location

trii asks for your location only while you use the app, and only to centre the map on nearby stations, order station suggestions by distance, and estimate where your train is during a trip. Your location is used on your device. trii does not send it to the developer, to your friends or to any data source, and keeps no location history. You can decline location access and still search for and save trips.

Maps, timetables and live train times

trii uses Apple's MapKit resources for the map, station search and journey times. It uses them as you use the app and stores none of their data: nothing from MapKit is kept by trii or sent to the developer. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

To show live train times in Great Britain, trii asks National Rail (Rail Data Marketplace) for departure information. To confirm that a place is a railway station, it asks OpenStreetMap (Overpass). These requests carry only station names, station codes, station coordinates and train service references. They carry nothing that identifies you.

The live status your device receives for your trip is kept with the trip, so that your friends see it without asking National Rail themselves.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's network address. The developer receives none of it.

Live Activities

The trip shown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is created and updated on your device. There is no push server. Your friends' trips can appear there too; you can turn this off for each friend in Manage Friend.

Apple and iCloud

Your trips, profile and shares are stored by Apple in iCloud, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to them.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you use trii through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms. They are used only to fix the app.

Children

trii collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Your data is on your device and in your iCloud, and you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • One trip: in My Trips or History, swipe left on the trip and tap Delete. It is deleted from your devices and your iCloud, and your friends no longer see it.
  • A friend: in Friends, tap the gear, choose the friend, and tap Remove Friend, or set their access to None.
  • Your name or picture: in your trii profile, clear your name, or tap your picture and choose Delete.
  • Everything in iCloud: deleting the trii app removes its data from that device, but not from your iCloud. To delete trii's data from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › trii, and choose Delete Data.
  • Access to location, contacts and the camera: go to Settings › trii.
  • A Live Activity: it ends on its own after the trip arrives, or you can swipe it away on the Lock Screen.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing for the developer to delete.
