The problem — Reliance only on the regulation leads into a larger indemnity spend. Besides, non consistent compensations can be used in courts to request a higher settlement.

The solution — A configurable endpoint that returns the most similar claims within the client database, compared to the market and adjusted to inflation.

The approach — Machine Learning K Nearest Neighbour Vector Algorithm that maps and finds the best claim matches and the compensation paid.
